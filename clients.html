CLIENT ACQUISITION

How to Find Clients

Learn different methods for finding your first client and developing a repeatable client-acquisition system.

Freelance Platforms

Search for projects and submit relevant proposals.

LinkedIn

Build your professional profile, network and contact potential clients.

Social Media

Share useful content and demonstrate your knowledge.

Direct Outreach

Find businesses that have a problem you can solve.

Referrals

Ask existing clients and professional contacts for referrals.

Communities

Participate in relevant professional communities without spamming members.

A Simple First-Client Process

01

Choose a Service

Know exactly what you are offering.

02

Define Your Client

Identify the type of person or business that needs your service.

03

Find Prospects

Use platforms, LinkedIn, social media, networking or outreach.

04

Study the Problem

Understand what the client actually needs.

05

Send a Relevant Message

Explain how you can help instead of sending generic spam.

06

Follow Up

Follow up professionally if the client does not respond.

What Makes a Good Proposal?

  • Show that you understood the client's problem.
  • Explain your relevant experience.
  • Describe how you would approach the task.
  • Ask important questions when necessary.
  • Keep the proposal focused and readable.
  • Do not make unrealistic promises.

What Not to Do

  • Do not send the same generic message to everyone.
  • Do not spam businesses repeatedly.
  • Do not claim experience you do not have.
  • Do not promise impossible deadlines.
  • Do not offer unlimited free work.
