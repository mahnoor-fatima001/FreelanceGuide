SHOW YOUR WORK

Build Your Portfolio

A portfolio gives potential clients evidence of what you can do.

What is a Portfolio?

A portfolio is a collection of your work that demonstrates your skills, problem-solving ability, experience and style.

If you are a beginner without paid clients, you can create your own projects to demonstrate your abilities.

What Should Your Portfolio Include?

About You

A short introduction explaining what you do.

Your Skills

List the services and technologies you actually understand.

Projects

Show your strongest and most relevant work.

Case Studies

Explain the problem, your process and the result.

Contact

Give potential clients a clear way to contact you.

Project Ideas

  • Business landing page
  • Restaurant website
  • Personal portfolio
  • Todo application
  • Expense tracker
  • Dashboard
  • E-commerce interface
  • Blog website
  • Social media campaign sample
  • Brand identity project

GitHub for Developers

Developers can use GitHub to publish source code and demonstrate projects. A strong GitHub profile can complement a personal portfolio.

Visit GitHub

Portfolio Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding projects you cannot explain.
  • Including too many unfinished projects.
  • Using copied projects without understanding them.
  • Having no clear contact method.
  • Using vague descriptions instead of explaining your work.
