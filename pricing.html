BUSINESS BASICS

Freelance Pricing

Learn the major pricing models and the factors that affect what you charge.

Hourly Pricing

You charge based on the amount of time spent working.

This can be useful when the project scope is difficult to predict.

Fixed Pricing

You charge an agreed amount for a clearly defined project.

The scope should be clearly documented.

Milestone Pricing

A larger project is divided into smaller stages.

Each milestone can have its own deliverable and payment.

What Should Influence Your Price?

Scope

How much work is actually required?

Complexity

How technically or creatively difficult is the project?

Deadline

Does the client require accelerated delivery?

Experience

Your experience and ability to deliver can influence pricing.

Revisions

How many revision rounds are included?

Platform Fees

Some platforms may charge fees that affect your net earnings.

Beginner Pricing Mistakes

  • Charging without understanding the scope.
  • Agreeing to unlimited revisions.
  • Accepting unrealistic deadlines.
  • Changing the project scope without discussing price.
  • Ignoring platform or payment fees.
  • Copying another freelancer's price without considering your own situation.

Remember

Your price should be connected to the service you provide, the scope of the work and the agreement with the client. There is no single universal price that works for every freelancer or every project.
