What is Freelancing?

Understand the freelance business model before trying to get your first client.

What is a Freelancer?

A freelancer is an independent worker who provides services to clients without necessarily becoming a permanent employee of those clients.

A freelancer may work with one client on a short project, several clients at the same time, or develop long-term relationships with recurring clients.

How Does Freelancing Work?

1. You Have a Skill

For example: web development, graphic design, video editing, writing or digital marketing.

2. A Client Has a Problem

The client needs something created, improved, managed or solved.

3. You Offer a Solution

You explain how your skill can solve the client's problem.

4. You Agree on Scope

Both sides agree on requirements, deadlines, revisions and payment.

5. You Deliver

You complete the agreed work and provide the deliverables.

6. You Get Paid

Payment depends on the agreement and platform or payment method being used.

Freelancer vs Employee

Freelancer Employee
Usually works independently Works for an employer
May work with multiple clients Usually has one employer
Often chooses projects Usually receives assigned responsibilities
Responsible for finding clients Employer generally provides work
Income can vary between projects Usually receives an agreed salary or wage

Advantages of Freelancing

  • Ability to work with clients from different locations.
  • Potential flexibility in choosing projects.
  • Opportunity to develop multiple skills.
  • Ability to build your own professional brand.
  • Potential to develop long-term client relationships.

Challenges of Freelancing

  • Finding clients can be difficult in the beginning.
  • Income may not be consistent.
  • You must manage your own deadlines.
  • You need to communicate professionally.
  • You need to handle your own marketing and portfolio.

What Do You Need to Start?

A Marketable Skill

Something useful that clients are willing to pay for.

Internet & Computer

A reliable setup for communicating and delivering work.

Portfolio

Examples that demonstrate your ability.

Communication

The ability to understand requirements and explain your work.
