WORK WITH CLIENTS

Client Communication

Technical ability is only one part of freelancing. You also need to understand requirements and communicate clearly.

Ask Questions

Clarify requirements before beginning work.

Confirm Scope

Make sure both sides understand what is included.

Set Deadlines

Agree on realistic delivery dates.

Give Updates

Keep clients informed about meaningful progress.

Handle Feedback

Treat reasonable revisions professionally.

Document Decisions

Keep important requirements and agreements in writing.

Before Starting a Project

  • What exactly needs to be delivered?
  • What is the deadline?
  • What files or information will the client provide?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • What is the agreed price?
  • How will the final work be delivered?

During the Project

  • Communicate important delays early.
  • Ask questions when requirements are unclear.
  • Don't disappear for days without explanation.
  • Keep backups of important project files.
  • Track requested changes.

After Delivery

  • Confirm that the client received the deliverables.
  • Ask whether everything meets the agreed requirements.
  • Resolve legitimate issues professionally.
  • Request feedback or a testimonial when appropriate.
  • Stay in touch for future opportunities.
