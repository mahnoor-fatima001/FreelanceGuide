Freelance Platforms

Different platforms use different models for connecting freelancers with clients.

Upwork

A large freelance marketplace where clients post projects and freelancers can submit proposals for suitable work.

Useful for: Development, design, writing, marketing, data, virtual assistance and many other services.

Visit Upwork →

Fiverr

A marketplace where freelancers can create service listings and clients can purchase or contact sellers.

Useful for: Design, video editing, writing, development, marketing and creative services.

Visit Fiverr →

Freelancer

A freelance marketplace where clients can post projects and freelancers can compete for available work.

Useful for: Development, design, writing, data and business services.

Visit Freelancer →

PeoplePerHour

A freelance platform connecting clients with freelancers for hourly or project-based work.

Useful for: Web development, design, writing, SEO, social media and business services.

Visit PeoplePerHour →

LinkedIn

A professional networking platform that can also be used to find freelance, contract and remote opportunities.

Useful for: Networking, direct outreach, job opportunities and building a professional reputation.

Find Jobs on LinkedIn →

Don't Depend on One Platform

Freelancers can combine marketplaces with networking, direct outreach, referrals and social media.

Freelance Marketplaces

Apply to projects and create service listings.

LinkedIn

Build relationships and contact potential clients professionally.

Social Media

Share useful content and demonstrate your expertise.

Referrals

Ask satisfied clients or professional contacts for referrals.

Direct Outreach

Identify businesses that may benefit from your service.
